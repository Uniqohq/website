# Uniqo product overview

Uniqo is an intelligent payment-card experience focused on direct, real-time control.

## Publicly described capabilities

- Virtual and physical cards
- Instant card freeze and unfreeze
- Configurable spending limits
- Real-time transaction notifications
- Virtual and one-time card numbers
- Merchant and country controls
- Time-based card rules
- Spending insights and AI spending categories
- Dynamic card numbers
- AI-assisted fraud protection

Uniqo is pre-launch. Features, eligibility and availability may change before release.
