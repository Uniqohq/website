# Uniqo frequently asked questions

## Is Uniqo live?

No. Uniqo is pre-launch and is currently collecting waitlist registrations.

## Is Uniqo a bank?

Uniqo describes itself as a financial technology product, not as another bank. Do not infer a banking license or regulatory approval from the website.

## What does Uniqo offer?

Uniqo is being designed around virtual and physical cards, real-time controls, spending insights, configurable payment rules and payment protection.

## Which plans are shown?

The public website shows Arctic, Midnight and Graphite. Prices and features are pre-launch information and may change.

## Where will Uniqo be available?

The website lists the United States, European Union, United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, Singapore, United Arab Emirates and Japan as planned regions. A listed region is not confirmation of current availability.

## Who owns Uniqo?

Uniqo is owned and operated by FrameLabs LLC.

## How can someone request access?

Join the waitlist at https://uniqo.one/waitlist.
