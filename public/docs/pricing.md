# Uniqo plans and pricing

## Arctic

Free. Includes a virtual card, one physical card, instant notifications, spending insights and freeze or unfreeze controls.

## Midnight

USD 4.99 per month or USD 48 per year. Includes Arctic features plus up to five virtual cards, instant card-number changes, one-time cards, AI spending categories and premium card designs.

## Graphite

USD 9.99 per month or USD 96 per year. Includes Midnight features plus unlimited virtual cards, merchant control, country lock, time-based card rules, AI fraud protection, a dynamic card number and travel insurance.

Prices and features are pre-launch information and may change. Joining the waitlist does not create a paid subscription.
