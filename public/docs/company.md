# About Uniqo

Uniqo is a pre-launch financial technology product owned and operated by FrameLabs LLC.

The product is built around a simple idea: give people clearer, faster control over how money moves without presenting another traditional bank experience. Uniqo is currently preparing access by region and is not yet publicly available.
