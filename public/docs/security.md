# Uniqo security overview

Uniqo is designed around controls that act before and immediately after a payment.

Publicly described controls include suspicious-payment checks, card freeze and unfreeze, instant notifications, configurable limits, merchant controls, country controls, time-based card rules, dynamic card numbers and AI-assisted fraud protection.

Uniqo is pre-launch. These descriptions are product information, not a guarantee against loss, fraud or unauthorized transactions.
